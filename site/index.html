Exploration / Atmospheric / Trick Coming to Steam

Lone Sands

Lone Sands is an exploration/atmospheric/trick game featuring wind-sail and kite riding across desert dunes, ocean waves and snow biomes. It has huge weather systems, and it is all around very fun to ride around and explore.

Idle / Action RPG Coming to Steam

Horizon of Steel

Horizon of Steel is an idle ARPG. Instead of direct control of combat, you build your character's skills, equipment and strategies, and send it into combat to farm or level. The fights play out on their own, drawn in ASCII.

Steam page ↗horizonofsteel.com ↗
It's a Montage↗
Pixel art / Training Coming to Steam

It's a Montage

It's a Montage is a pixel-art training game with no opponents, where every level you gain shows on your character and in the world around you. Once you outgrow the dojo, the sensei sends you to train at a waterfall, on the open sea, in an ocean trench, on the moon, on a star and at the edge of a black hole.

itsamontage.com ↗Press kit ↗
Odyssos↗
Incremental / Browser Out now, free

Odyssos

Odyssos is a free incremental game you play in your browser. It starts with basic training and builds into exploring world maps, climbing a tournament and rebirths that keep permanent upgrades, eventually all the way out into space.

Play in your browser ↗On itch.io ↗

The studio

Made by
one developer.

Odyssos Studios is an independent game studio founded by Adam Bandel.

Lone Sands is about riding and exploring, Horizon of Steel is about building a character and its strategies, It's a Montage is about training, and Odyssos is about growing your power level from basic training out into space.

Each game has its own site with more about it, and the Odyssos Discord server covers all of them.
