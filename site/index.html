The studio

Different worlds.
A personal approach.

Odyssos Studios is an independent game studio founded by Adam Bandel.

Our games take different paths: Lone Sands is about movement and exploration; Horizon of Steel is about character builds and watching your decisions play out; It's a Montage is about training until the world can't hold you; Odyssos is a long climb from first training to the cosmos.

This is where you'll find our games, their latest release details, and how to get in touch.
